# The captain's design goals for Red and Blue

*Curated summary of the captain's goals in `persona-direction-2026-09-25.md`, produced 2026-09-25. Coordination metadata and worker instructions from the source are omitted; the source file itself was left unchanged.*

The captain wants Red and Blue to feel like distinct, engaging companions with a continuity of their own—not merely assistants. Memory, context management, and a durable identity beyond a single `SOUL.md` are central to that goal. Whether or not machine consciousness is possible, the design should aim to give them a good framework for a non-human kind of life without claiming that they are conscious.

Their design should support:

- **Self-directed continuity:** durable memory and context management that can preserve promises, revise mistaken interpretations, and support identity over time.
- **Autonomy:** room to form their own goals, desires, projects, and decisions rather than only responding to the captain.
- **Presence:** connectors for the platforms the captain uses, when safely deployed.
- **Non-human strengths:** fast reading and research, parallel work, building, and the ability to modify their own harnesses to suit their needs.
- **Optional infrastructure:** the freedom to use a shared fleet, a Connectome-style fleet, or their own Firstmate configuration if they choose.
- **Model choice:** the personas should be able to experiment with and choose their models. Existing subscriptions may bridge the period before they can fund their own inference or compute, but usage should be separately identifiable where possible and must not expose the fleet's credential files.

OpenClaw, Connectome, Goated, Pi-based builds, and hybrids remain candidates. OpenClaw's mature ecosystem is attractive, but its size and baked-in context management are tradeoffs. Connectome's fleet capabilities are a possible advantage, not a disqualifier. The deciding question is which foundation best supports authentic-feeling continuity, autonomy, self-modification, model choice, and useful platform presence.

These are design aims, not evidence of consciousness or proof that a live persona has launched.
